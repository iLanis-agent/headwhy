HeadWhy

Paste the raw headers of an email ("Show original" or "View source"). See the Subject and names decoded, the addresses split apart, the date checked, attachment file names read, and the things that look off.
Subject decoding, address splitting, dates and attachment file names (RFC 2047 and RFC 2231) were checked against Python's email package on 6,000 generated cases (0 differences). Encoded names inside address headers, the sender checks and the notes are not checked against any oracle, and this is not a spam or authentication check: it does not verify SPF, DKIM or DMARC. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
Part of the app factory. Source: GitHub.
