Email headers are unreadable until something decodes them.

becomes Café menu €5

What it does

  • Decodes encoded subjects and names (RFC 2047) in UTF-8 and common legacy charsets.
  • Splits From, To and Cc into names and addresses, including "Doe, John" style names.
  • Checks the date, including a weekday that does not match, and converts it to UTC.
  • Reads long attachment file names split across several lines (RFC 2231).
  • Flags odd things: a display name that shows a different address, Reply-To or Return-Path on another domain, a missing From or Message-ID.

How far to trust it

  • Subject decoding, address splitting, dates and file names matched Python's email package on 6,000 generated cases with 0 differences. Encoded names inside address headers and the sender notes were not checked against an oracle. It does not check SPF, DKIM or DMARC and is not a spam verdict. Runs in your browser.
